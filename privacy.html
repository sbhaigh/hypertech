Solar Explorer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Overview

Solar Explorer does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location services.

Data Used by the App

The app requests planetary position data from NASA/JPL Horizons so it can place solar system bodies accurately when that service is available. Those requests are used only to retrieve ephemeris data and do not include your name, email address, precise device location, contacts, or other personal profile data.

Local Preferences

Solar Explorer stores your audio mute preference locally on your device. This preference is not transmitted by the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@hyperte.ch.
