Product

Arcade flight through a living solar system.

Solar Explorer combines real planetary scale references, NASA/JPL ephemeris data when available, manual flight controls, proximity protection, asteroid fields, and a cockpit-style navigation arc.

Manual Navigation

Fly with responsive controls and use the planet compass to orient toward nearby worlds without relying on autopilot.

Solar System Context

Distances, headings, orbit guides, and minimap cues help keep exploration readable at interplanetary scale.

Built for iPhone

Compact controls, audio defaults, and release-focused UI details are tuned for handheld play and App Store distribution.
