Mission briefing

Solar Explorer at a glance.

Quick answers about the app, the 2.1 update, and supported Apple devices.

What is Solar Explorer?

Solar Explorer is an interactive solar system app by HyperTech. Pilot a spacecraft, visit planets and moons, study planet facts, browse discovery galleries, and explore lunar history.

What is new in version 2.1?

Version 2.1 adds planetary briefings, discovery galleries, an Apollo archive, smoother approaches and departures, enhanced haptics, refined navigation and time controls, and improved cockpit layouts.

Which devices are supported?

Solar Explorer is designed for iPhone and iPad, with responsive cockpit layouts that adapt the flight, navigation, and discovery experience to different screen sizes.
